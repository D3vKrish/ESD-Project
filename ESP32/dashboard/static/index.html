Push alerts · ntfy

Get a notification on your phone when the current leaves the threshold range. Install the free ntfy app and subscribe to the topic below. No account needed.

—
Topic
Anyone who knows the topic can read it, so use something hard to guess. Letters, digits, - and _ only.
Subscribe on your phone
In the ntfy app tap + and enter the topic , or open this link in a browser to get notifications there too.
How to set up (1 min)
  1. Install ntfy from the Play Store, App Store or F-Droid.
  2. Press Generate above (or type your own topic), then Save.
  3. In the app, tap + and subscribe to the same topic.
  4. Press Send test notification. It should appear on your phone within a few seconds.

Everyone subscribed to the topic gets the alerts, so a whole team can share it.
